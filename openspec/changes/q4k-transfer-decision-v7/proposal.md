# Change: Q4_K Transfer Gate and Claim Policy V7

## Why

Successful representation reconstruction and successful optimization are distinct
research outcomes. Regional ties must not become model-level throughput claims.

## What Changes

- Add ordered transfer classifications and mandatory stop gates.
- Publish rigorous negative results with causal evidence.
- Separate regenerated-baseline model verification from candidate integration.

## Success

Reports state exactly which gates passed, which failed, what was not run, and why no
tokens/sec claim exists when regional transfer fails.
