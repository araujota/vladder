# Change: SKSF Attribution Gate V6

## Why

V5 could add theoretically plausible grammar rules without proving that their target cost
was material in the production workload. This makes search growth outrun practical value.

## What Changes

- Add immutable attribution studies and bottleneck identities.
- Require evidence-backed grammar admission before production search.
- Distinguish admitted, exploratory, and rejected families.

## Success

No grammar family enters default search without a hash-validated measured bottleneck and
metric-aligned optimization hypothesis.
