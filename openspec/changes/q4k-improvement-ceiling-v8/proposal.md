# Change: Q4_K Improvement Ceiling and Grammar Admission V8

## Why

Broad diagnostic envelopes can justify more measurement but cannot by themselves prove
that an optimization is recoverable or admit an unbounded grammar.

## What Changes

- Calculate conservative and optimistic scenario ceilings without converting them into wins.
- Require attributable cost, plausible value, E1 tractability, and distinctness.
- Classify each family as admit, defer, reject, or requires-more-measurement.

## Success

Exactly the transformations supported by measured evidence are promoted, with a bounded
first experiment and explicit promotion gate.
